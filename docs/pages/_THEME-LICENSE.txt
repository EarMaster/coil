The layouts, includes, stylesheets and the Google Play badge in this folder are derived from
the "Automatic App Landing Page" Jekyll theme:

    https://github.com/emilbaehr/automatic-app-landing-page

The theme is vendored rather than referenced as a remote_theme, so its MIT licence notice is
reproduced here in full, as that licence requires. Coil itself is GPL-3.0 (see /LICENSE at the
repo root) — the two do not conflict; MIT is compatible with the GPL.

The filename starts with an underscore so Jekyll treats it as a special entry and keeps it out
of the built site. It is a notice for people reading the repo, not a page.
